## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an encouraging, high-agency career intelligence platform. The visual tone balances modern consumer warmth with clinical evaluation metrics: candid, empathetic, structured, and goal-oriented. Rather than sterile corporate blue or cold technical gray, the UI adopts soft, inviting warm alabaster and milk-cream surfaces grounded by crisp charcoal typography, electrified by punchy coral-orange accents.

The aesthetic philosophy bridges **Playful Tactility** and **Modern Soft Minimalism**:
- **Generous pill geometry and rounded card canvases** that eliminate intimidation from high-stress scenarios like mock interviews and rubric analysis.
- **High-legibility structured analytics**, using micro-badges, inline metric counters, and crisp score breakdowns.
- **Selective inverted depth**: Deep charcoal night-mode breakout sections for data-heavy executive reviews and real-time audio analysis modules, paired with breezy, sunlit daytime surfaces for coaching tracks and setup flows.
- **Warm, welcoming interaction states** that feel humane, optimistic, and supportive.

## Colors

The color palette centers around an energetic, humanistic balance between warm earthy bases and high-chroma action tones.

- **Primary (`#EB5A3C`)**: Vivid Coral Red/Orange. Reserved for key actions, live recording states, primary CTAs, positive highlight badges, and critical interactive drivers.
- **Secondary (`#121621`)**: Deep Obsidian Slate. Used for high-contrast hero typography, primary surface inverting, pill tabs, and high-impact structural framing.
- **Tertiary (`#F5A623`)**: Warm Amber. Drives score warning tags, secondary milestones, and comparative progress rings.
- **Neutral (`#FDF8EE`)**: Warm Eggshell / Cream. Establishes the foundational canvas, replacing stark clinical whites with an organic, low-fatigue background.

### Semantic & Inversion Logic
- Dark-mode breakout sections (`#101319`) are applied contextually to complex analytical dashboards, audio waveform visualizers, and simulated camera stages. Inside these blocks, surface tiers step up to `#1A1F2C`, and border strokes switch to `#272E3F`.
- Positive feedback uses `metric-green` (`#10B981`), wrapped in soft background tints (`#E8F8F2`).

## Typography

The type hierarchy uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to project an approachable, geometric warmth with crisp clarity in compact data layouts.

- **Headlines & Display**: Set with tight negative letter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (700/800) to create a bold, editorial feel that anchors each section.
- **Body Text**: Tuned for effortless readability against warm off-white tones, adhering to comfortable line heights (1.5x–1.6x) and medium-contrast charcoal shades.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Compact, uppercase or semi-bold micro-labels are utilized for pill tags, category badges, and metric units to maintain strong structural scannability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout uses a **fluid-responsive 12-column grid** anchored by clean outer bounds and airy, breathing section intervals.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: Max content container of 1200px. Sections use `space-xl` (2.5rem) to `4rem` vertical breathing room. Grids utilize 12 columns with a 24px (`1.5rem`) gutter.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout. Horizontal margin contracts to 2rem, collapsing 3- and 4-column card groups into clean 2-column matrices.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with 1.25rem outer edge margins. Analytics dashboards switch to stacked or horizontally swipable card groups.

Spacing tokens prioritize functional grouping: use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for badge internals and icon-label pairings, `space-md` (16px) for card body padding and item clusters, and `space-lg` (24px) for distinct card separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal layer differentiation combined with soft, warm ambient drop shadows**. High-contrast hard shadows are avoided in favor of gentle, diffusion-based floating planes.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas)**: Baseline cream canvas (`#FDF8EE`) or inverted slate (`#101319`).
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card faces layered over the warm canvas, bounded by a featherweight border (`1px solid #EDE6D8`) and a delicate ambient shadow (`0 2px 8px -2px rgba(18, 22, 33, 0.04), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(18, 22, 33, 0.06)`).
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Tooltips & Active Cards)**: Elevated white cards with expanded diffusion (`0 12px 32px -4px rgba(18, 22, 33, 0.1)`).
- **Level 3 (Floating Action Bars & Modals)**: Concentrated floating depth (`0 20px 48px -8px rgba(18, 22, 33, 0.14)`).

In dark-mode sections, elevation relies entirely on progressive tonal step-ups (`#101319` -> `#1A1F2C` -> `#252C3D`) paired with subtle top-highlight borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Pill & High-Curvature Rounding** philosophy (`roundedness: 3`). 

- **Pill Geometry (`rounded-full` / `9999px`)**: Standard for all primary and secondary buttons, status badges, metric chips, filter tags, and navigational segmented controls.
- **Containers & Big Cards (`rounded-3xl` / `1.5rem` to `2rem`)**: Applied to hero visual stages, feature overview panels, testimonial containers, and comparison grids.
- **Compact Cards & Sub-modules (`rounded-2xl` / `1rem`)**: Applied to inner metric readouts, question prompt boxes, and interactive list elements.
- **Inputs & Dropdowns (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-full`)**: Form controls maintain soft 12px to 16px corner radii to match button curvature.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Button**: Solid vibrant coral (`#EB5A3C`), text in `#FFFFFF`, full pill shape (`rounded-full`). Generous horizontal padding (`px-7 py-3.5`). Hover shifts to `#D84A2E` with a slight upward lift.
- **Secondary Button**: Solid deep obsidian (`#121621`), text in `#FFFFFF`, full pill shape. Hover shifts to `#1F2637`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Button**: Transparent background, crisp `#121621` or `#EDE6D8` border, text in `#121621`. Hover fills with `#F7F2E7`.

### Badges, Tags & Chips
- **Category & Feature Badges**: High-contrast pill badges. Backgrounds use pastel tints (`#FFF0ED` for coral, `#E8F8F2` for green) paired with bold saturated text matching the hue.
- **Segmented Control Tabs**: Enclosed pill pills. A container of `#F0EADE` with an active tab pill of `#121621` (white text) or pure `#FFFFFF` (dark text).

### Cards & Analytical Containers
- **Content Cards**: White background, `rounded-3xl`, thin `#EDE6D8` border, Level 1 ambient shadow.
- **Metric Cards**: Include a top or inline pill badge, large bold metric value (`headline-lg`), contextual sub-label (`body-sm`), and a horizontal or circular progression bar.
- **Inverted Dark Stage**: `#101319` background with `rounded-3xl`, housing high-tech interactive experiences like live audio analysis, video mock interview stages, and feedback checklists.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Search & Input Bars**: Generous pill shape (`rounded-full`) or `rounded-xl`, soft cream or white fill, subtle border (`#EDE6D8`). Focused state activates a distinct 2px ring in primary coral (`#EB5A3C`) with 0px blur offset.
- **Radio & Checkboxes**: Rounded-full radio circles and `rounded-md` check containers. Active states fill with `#EB5A3C` accompanied by clean white iconography.

### Progress Meters & Analytics
- Track lines use soft neutral sands (`#EDE6D8`), filled with gradient-free solid primary coral or metric green to ensure instantaneous visual comprehension.
